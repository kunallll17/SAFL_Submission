WHAT THE ATTACKER MAY KNOW

              
All of the rules.

              

                Prompts, templates, policy code, schemas and the public key can
                be public. The attacker may craft any valid tool proposal,
                invent an explanation, and alter or replay an execution request.
              

              

                Authorization rests on separate owner credentials and a private
                signing key, not secret prompts. Field provenance is derived
                from stored user constraints; an agent cannot label its own
                output “trusted.”
              

            

            

              
WHAT WE DO NOT CLAIM

              
A proof of understanding.

              

                A direction choice detects a mismatching answer. It does not
                prove someone understood the transaction or prevent a person
                from being coached into the correct answer.
              

              

                A compromised browser, stolen owner session, compromised signing
                service or malicious local process is outside this prototype’s
                boundary. Production requires isolated origins, stronger
                identity, secure key storage and user studies.
              

            

            

              
DATA SHARING

              
Consent ends at execution.

              

                Document scope, purpose and retention are bound to the approved
                request. This synthetic gateway cannot force a recipient to
                delete copied data or honor a stated purpose.
