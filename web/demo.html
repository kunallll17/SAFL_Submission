How VaaniVerify works
A suggestion is not permission.
The agent proposes. You choose. A separate gateway checks.
01
You set the permission
Choose what the agent may do, who it may send to and the exact limits. These fields define the permission; the wording of a message cannot override them.
02
Review the actual action
A local model proposes an action. VaaniVerify compares it with your permission and describes the action using fixed templates in English, Hindi, Hinglish or Tamil.
03
Approve exactly once
Your approval signs the exact action. A separate gateway rejects changed payloads and reused approvals before recording a simulated result.
A demo with clear limits
No real UPI payments or document sharing. The model can be wrong. A confirmation is not proof that someone understood it.
This prototype does not protect a compromised device or a stolen owner session.
